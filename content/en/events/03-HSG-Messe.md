---
title: Student Group Fair
date: November 3–5
who: StuVe
where: A-Foyer
---
From November 3 to 5, some of the more than 60 student groups at the University of Konstanz will have a booth in the A-Foyer.
Stop by, get to know the groups, join in on games and puzzles, and strike up conversations with other students!

You can also find a list of all of them [on our website](https://www.stuve-uni-kn.de/hochschulgruppen/thematische-hochschulgruppen/).
