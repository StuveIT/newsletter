A mystical atmosphere, exciting stories, picturesque alleyways: On the night watchman’s tour, you’ll discover Konstanz in a whole new light. The night watchman’s entertaining anecdotes and songs during this discovery tour through “Niederburg”—Konstanz’s oldest neighborhood—will round out a wonderful evening or kick it off.

Duration: 1.5 hours

Participation fee: €5

**Note: The tour is conducted in German only.**

We subsidize the tour, so it’s significantly cheaper than if you booked it directly. **Please register [online](https://pretix.eu/stuve-ukn/stadtfuehrung/) in advance.** We’ll collect the payment in cash during the tour.
