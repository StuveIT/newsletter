This accessible lecture offers fascinating insights into nature—from the tiniest worlds inside atoms to the vastness of the universe and the intriguing question of its origin. Stunning images and fascinating statistics leave us in awe, prompting the question: "Where did it all come from?"

Physicist Thomas Schimmel conducts research and teaches as a university professor at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT). He is the director of the Center for Single-Atom Technologies and a co-founder of the Institute for Nanotechnology. He has repeatedly been honored with prestigious awards for his research achievements.
