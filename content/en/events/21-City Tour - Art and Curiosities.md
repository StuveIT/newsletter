Playful statues and hidden figures: The alleys of Konstanz are home to numerous, sometimes curious works of art. On this walking tour, participants will discover the stories behind these special places and encounter a courtesan from centuries past, a provocative triumphal arch, gnomes, medieval texters, and many other curiosities.

Duration: 1.5 hours

Participation fee: €4

**Note: The tour is conducted in German only.**

We subsidize the tour, so it’s significantly cheaper than if you booked it directly. **Please register [online](https://pretix.eu/stuve-ukn/stadtfuehrung/) in advance.** We’ll collect the payment in cash during the tour.
