Are you starting college this year, and does the campus still feel like a maze to you?  
Sign up for the campus tour!

In 1.5 hours, we’ll show you the entire University of Konstanz campus—from Seezeit, the cafeteria, and university sports to helpful tips on campus life! You’ll get practical advice for starting your studies and have the chance to meet a few new faces. Afterward, we’ll head to the StuVe room, where snacks, drinks, and interesting conversations await you. Feel free to ask questions!

Please sign up [online](https://pretix.eu/stuve-ukn/unifuehrung/) for a campus tour.
