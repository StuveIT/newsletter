---
title: Welcome Event
date: october 12
who: University of Konstanz - Office of the President
where: A600
time: "4 pm"
---

The university cordially invites all new students to the official welcome ceremony hosted by the Office of the President.

Afterward, small teams and individuals are invited to participate in the campus rally. Details on how to access the event will be announced at the end of the welcome ceremony.

Starting at 5:30 p.m., there will be a get-together with refreshments and information booths on A5 and the N-P terrace (beer garden).

We, the Student Council, will also be there and would be happy to answer your questions!

You can find more information about the welcome event and other events offered by the university [on the university website](https://www.uni-konstanz.de/en/study/before-you-study/guide-for-new-students/orientation-weeks/).
