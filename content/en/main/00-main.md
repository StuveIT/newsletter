We warmly welcome all freshmen at the University of Konstanz and hope you all had a wonderful summer and a great start to the semester. This newsletter provides an overview of upcoming events—especially, but not exclusively, for freshmen. So feel free to stop by—we look forward to seeing you there!

## News

- **🛋️ Got a Sofa?**  
  Do you have a couch, mattress, or other place to sleep available? Or are you still looking for a place to stay at the start of the semester?

  With our “Got a Sofa?” campaign, we—the Department for Sustainability, Social Affairs & Equality (NaSoGl) of the Student Council (StuVe)—want to help freshmen and other students get off to a good start in Konstanz.

  After all, finding a place to live is especially difficult right at the start of the semester. A temporary place to stay can make a big difference.

  Can you offer a place to stay, or are you looking for a place yourself? Then simply sign up [on our website](https://www.stuve-uni-kn.de/haste-maln-sofa/registrierung/). We’ll match you with the right people and get in touch with you.

  **Important: Offers must be free of charge and reliable.**

  Do you have any questions? Email us at stuve.nasogl@uni-konstanz.de with the subject line \[HMS\].

  You can find more information about the campaign [on our website](https://www.stuve-uni-kn.de/2026/09/07/haste-maln-sofa-geht-in-die-naechste-runde-2/).

  Let's work together to make sure new students in Konstanz get off to a great start in college! 🫶🏼
- **🎨 Artists Wanted for the Arche**  
  The former Asian bistro “Arche” is being transformed into a cozy lounge and event space for students. The interior design and concept already set it apart from the rest of the campus. Now all that’s missing is what truly brings a space to life: art on the walls.

  That’s why we’re looking for creative minds and hands among you. All students, artists, collectives, and initiatives are welcome to participate. Up to 100 € in material costs will be reimbursed.

  ❗️Submission deadline: October 31, 2026

  Are you interested in joining? You can find more information [on our website](https://cloud.uni-konstanz.de/index.php/apps/files/files/www.stuve-uni-kn.de/ausschreibung-bunte-arche/).

- **☕ StuVe Café**  
  The StuVe Café takes place every Thursday afternoon in H301a.

  Come join us at the StuVe office in H301a between 12:30 p.m. and 2:30 p.m. for free coffee, tea—with oat milk and ice cubes upon request—and cookies (including vegan options)!

  We look forward to seeing you!

## Who are we?

We are the Student Council at the University of Konstanz (StuVe). We represent students’ interests and organize and support projects. We’re here for you if you have any problems or concerns—or even if you’d like to get involved. Feel free to reach out to us—you can even do so [anonymously on our website](https://www.stuve-uni-kn.de/beschwerde/)!
